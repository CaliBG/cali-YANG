YetZeroSense

We reject unnecessary rhetoric and speak only of existence itself. YZS is a blank canvas for young souls — a youth visual brand exploring the boundary between "selflessness" and "being".

We live in an era of "over-perception". Emotions are amplified by algorithms, colors are symbolized, and even "authenticity" is packaged as a commodity. YZS offers no answers — only blank space.
