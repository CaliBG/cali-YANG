01 / THE IDEA

Why capture only one side?

When we film the scene, our own reaction disappears. When we turn the camera around, the moment has already passed.

Tongkuang begins with a simple idea: keep the person filming in the story. Three clear entry points bring dual-camera capture, screen recording and spoken storytelling onto one device.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Three modes. One familiar flow.

Switch modes to explore each way of creating.

The scene. Your reaction. One take.

Bring front and back perspectives into a single capture. Picture-in-picture, stacked and split layouts let the composition follow the story.
