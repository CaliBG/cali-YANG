A file may be saved locally but unavailable on another device; an upload may have reached a server without producing a receipt visible to its sender. This theoretical paper examines completion as a relationship between feedback, a particular version of an artefact, and a subsequent user action. A focused discussion of human–computer interaction research on progress, closure, synchronisation, recovery, and task resumption motivates an action-relative account. The resulting design guidance concerns scoped completion messages, recoverable outcomes, and access to evidence after interruption.
