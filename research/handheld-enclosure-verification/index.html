From Fit Models to Sealing Evidence in a Thin Handheld Enclosure

A staged CAD verification method for assembly fit, connector alignment and seal geometry in a thin handheld enclosure.

Journal
International Journal on Interactive Design and Manufacturing
Author
Zishuo Yang
Submitted
2026-11-12
Status
Submitted to journal

Status checked 2026-11-13

Journal website ↗

Research question

What can digital geometry establish about a thin enclosure, and which uncertainties still need a physical prototype?

Method

Across three design revisions, the study separates printable fit models, component layout and a rear-seal model. It audits exported meshes, re-imports assembly STEP files, calculates tolerance sensitivity and checks the connector against the frame.

Key findings

Early print files checked
12
Later print layouts checked
2
Valid assembly STEP re-imports
2
  • Twelve early print files and two later print layouts had closed, consistently wound meshes. Both assembly STEP exports re-imported as valid geometry.
  • The gap sensitivity calculation widened the estimated gasket squeeze range. The connector study also found that zero overlap could coincide with contact.

Limitations

The results describe CAD geometry and calculated sensitivity. Ingress resistance, strength and electrical performance still need physical testing.
