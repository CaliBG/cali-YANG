02 / VISUAL IDENTITY

A symbol becomes a system

The constructed V, palette and typography establish the identity. A 12-column grid gives every page a consistent structure. Separate 38-page Chinese and English editions bring colour and logo misuse onto one page each, preserving room for interfaces and widgets while adding platform type, action states, contrast and production guidance.

03 / INTERFACE & WIDGETS

The numbers that matter at a glance

Prices lead, asset rows stay compact and actions have room to breathe. Widgets are a central expression of the system: wide, square, watchlist and lock-screen formats share the same hierarchy and chart language.

Numbers on one side trends on the other

A 38.2 / 61.8 split balances figures and charts in the wide widget. Square widgets focus on a single asset, keeping every size part of the same product.
